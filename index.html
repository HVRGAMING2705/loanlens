LoanLens

Loan Approval Predictor — logistic regression, trained on 900 synthetic loan applications. All prediction happens in your browser; no data leaves this page.

Enter the application details and press Predict approval.

Model: 5-fold cross-validated accuracy = 83.6%, AUC = 0.91 (always guessing the majority class: 54.7% — about 1.5× better). A chance above 50% is flagged as approval likely. Trained on synthetic data for learning purposes; real loan decisions also use verified documents, bank statements and bank policy.
